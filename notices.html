AP-10K

Cherrae's animal-analysis model was trained using adapted portions of AP-10K: A Benchmark for Animal Pose Estimation in the Wild by Hang Yu, Yufei Xu, Jing Zhang, Wei Zhao, Ziyu Guan, and Dacheng Tao (NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks Track, 2021).

  • Source: AP-10K repository
  • License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  • Changes: Cherrae selected cat and dog examples and annotations, applied training-time transformations including rotation and resizing, added separate negative examples, trained new model weights, and converted and quantized those weights for on-device inference.
