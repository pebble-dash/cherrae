Cherrae Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026
Developer: Candy Rocks
App: Cherrae for Android and iOS

Overview

This policy applies to Cherrae for Android and iOS. Cherrae helps you select, group, arrange, save, and share photos. Photo analysis runs on your device. Candy Rocks does not operate servers that receive your photos, analysis results, selections, or working sessions. Cherrae does not contain advertising, require an account, or use developer-operated analytics.

Photos and on-device analysis

You choose which photos Cherrae may access through your device's system picker. Where folder selection is available, Cherrae reads supported image files directly inside the chosen folder and does not load images from its subfolders. Cherrae may read up to 2,500 images from a selected folder.

Cherrae analyzes visual body-position and posture estimates derived from selected photos to bring similar human and animal shots together. This processing occurs on your device. The resulting measurements are temporary: they are kept in memory while needed for your current work and are not uploaded or added to Cherrae's saved working-session record. Your original photos are never deleted or modified by Cherrae.

Cherrae uses this information only to organize photos. It does not use face recognition or facial identity features, identify or authenticate people, track individuals across photos, create identity profiles or templates, or make medical, employment, insurance, credit, legal, or other consequential decisions.

Information stored on your device

Cherrae stores a private working-session record containing references to selected photos and your choices so you can resume your work. Using Start over clears that record and releases any persisted folder access. Clearing Cherrae's app data through device settings or uninstalling Cherrae removes its private app data, subject to any device or cloud backup settings you control.

Saving and sharing

When you choose Save to Photos, Cherrae creates ordered copies in your device's photo library. Free-tier copies include a small Cherrae mark; Cherrae Pro copies do not. Saved copies remain until you delete them using your photo library or file manager.

When you choose Share set, Cherrae creates temporary copies in its private cache and gives the receiving app you select temporary permission to read them. Free-tier shared copies include the Cherrae mark; Cherrae Pro copies do not. Temporary copies are replaced by a later share and are removed when the cache is cleared or Cherrae is uninstalled. After you choose another app or service, that recipient's privacy practices apply.

Service providers and store purchases

On Android, Cherrae uses Google ML Kit for on-device photo analysis. Google states that ML Kit does not send input images or analysis results to its servers. The SDK does collect limited app and device information, per-installation identifiers, feature-usage events, performance information, and diagnostics for analytics and troubleshooting. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties. Pose detection also uses Google Firebase services for remote-configuration diagnostics. Google describes this collection here.

The optional Cherrae Pro unlock is processed by the store through which you obtained Cherrae, such as Google Play or Apple's App Store. Cherrae receives the purchase status and transaction information needed to activate, acknowledge, and restore the unlock. Candy Rocks does not receive or store your payment-card details. The store provider processes the transaction under its own privacy terms.

Data sharing and sale

Candy Rocks does not sell personal information. Candy Rocks does not share your photos, selections, or analysis results. Limited diagnostic data is processed by Google on Android as described above. Information is also transferred when you deliberately choose a receiving app through your device's share interface.

Retention and deletion

Candy Rocks does not maintain a server-side Cherrae account or database of your photos or sessions. You control locally saved photos and Cherrae's on-device data. Use Start over, clear the app's data where your operating system provides that option, or uninstall Cherrae to remove local app data, subject to backup settings you control. Google and Apple control retention of information they process under their respective policies.

Security

Cherrae uses operating-system access controls and system pickers to limit photo access to what you choose. Data Cherrae or its included services transmit is sent using encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but Cherrae is designed to minimize collection and keep photo processing local.

Children

Cherrae is not directed to children under 13, and Candy Rocks does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Cherrae.

Changes to this policy

Candy Rocks may update this policy when Cherrae's features, dependencies, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be updated when changes are published.
