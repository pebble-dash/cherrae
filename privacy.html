Cherrae Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026
Developer: Candy Rocks
App identifier: cherrae.pics

Overview

Cherrae helps you select, group, arrange, save, and share photos. Photo analysis runs on your device. Candy Rocks does not operate servers that receive your photos, pose descriptors, selections, or working sessions. Cherrae does not contain advertising, require an account, or use developer-operated analytics.

Photos and on-device analysis

You choose which photos or folder Cherrae may access through Android's system pickers. When you select a folder, Cherrae reads supported image files directly inside that folder and does not load images from its subfolders. Cherrae may read up to 2,500 images from a selected folder.

Cherrae analyzes selected photos on your device to group similar body poses. It does not upload image content or pose-detection results to Candy Rocks or Google. Your original photos are never deleted or modified by Cherrae.

Cherrae does not use face recognition, identify people, create identity templates, or use body measurements to establish identity. Temporary pose descriptors contain normalized joint coordinates and angles used only to group photos from the selected set. These descriptors are not saved in the resumable session.

Information stored on your device

Cherrae stores a private working-session record containing references to selected photos and your choices so you can resume your work. Using Start over clears that record and releases persisted folder access. Android's Clear storage command or uninstalling Cherrae clears its private app data.

Saving and sharing

When you choose Save to Photos, Cherrae creates ordered copies in a local Pictures/Cherrae album. Free-tier copies include a small Cherrae mark; Cherrae Pro copies do not. Saved copies remain until you delete them using your gallery or file manager.

When you choose Share set, Cherrae creates temporary copies in its private cache and gives the receiving app you select temporary permission to read them. Free-tier shared copies include the Cherrae mark; Cherrae Pro copies do not. Temporary copies are replaced by a later share and are removed when the cache is cleared or Cherrae is uninstalled. After you choose another app or service, that recipient's privacy practices apply.

Google ML Kit

Cherrae uses Google ML Kit's bundled pose-detection library for on-device image processing. Google states that ML Kit does not send input images or pose-detection outputs to its servers. ML Kit sends limited SDK diagnostics and usage metrics to Google, including device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature events, and error codes. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. Pose detection also uses Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations for remote-configuration diagnostics. See Google's ML Kit data-disclosure documentation.

Cherrae Pro and Google Play

The optional Cherrae Pro lifetime unlock is processed by Google Play as a one-time purchase. Cherrae receives purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play to activate and restore the unlock. Candy Rocks does not receive or store your payment-card details. Google's privacy terms apply to the transaction.

Data sharing and sale

Candy Rocks does not sell personal information. Candy Rocks does not share your photos, selections, or pose results. Limited ML Kit diagnostic data is processed by Google as described above. Information is also transferred when you deliberately choose a receiving app through Android's share interface.

Retention and deletion

Candy Rocks does not maintain a server-side Cherrae account or database of your photos or sessions. You control locally saved photos and Cherrae's on-device data. Use Start over, Android's Clear storage, or uninstall the app to remove local app data. Google controls retention of information processed by Google Play, ML Kit, Firebase Remote Config, and Firebase Installations under Google's policies.

Security

Cherrae uses Android's scoped system pickers instead of requesting broad access to your photo library. Its network traffic is restricted to encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but Cherrae is designed to minimize collection and keep photo processing local.

Children

Cherrae is not directed to children under 13, and Candy Rocks does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Cherrae.

Changes to this policy

Candy Rocks may update this policy when Cherrae's features, dependencies, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be updated when changes are published.
